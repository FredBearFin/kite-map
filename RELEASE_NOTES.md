First test build of Kite Map.

- **KiteMap-…-setup.exe**: installer (no admin needed).
- **KiteMap-…-portable.zip**: unzip and run `kite-map.exe`.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app is not code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

To start: open the Regions tab, click Draw area, drag a box, and click Download. Then check the Spots tab.
