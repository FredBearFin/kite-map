        How spots are scored

        Each lake gets 0 to 100. For each of 16 wind directions it checks the longest clear run of water along the wind (1.5 km or more is ideal) and the width across it (800 m or more). Those are weighted by how many daylight hours per year the wind blows 12 to 35 knots from that direction, using last year's hourly ERA5 weather data.
